# Emergency Protocols

## Immediate Life-Threatening Emergencies
- **Action**: Call 911 immediately.
- **Situations**: Chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, sudden weakness or numbness in face/arm/leg (signs of stroke), loss of consciousness.

## Medical Non-Emergencies (Urgent Care)
- **Action**: Go to the nearest Urgent Care or call your primary care doctor's emergency line.
- **Situations**: Minor cuts needing stitches, sprains, mild fevers, urinary tract infections.

## Falls at Home
- **Action**: Do not panic. If you are hurt and cannot get up, shout for help, use a medical alert button if you have one, or reach for a phone to call 911. 
- **If you can get up safely**: Roll onto your side, get on your hands and knees, crawl to a sturdy chair, and slowly lift yourself up.

## Power Outages
- **Action**: Keep flashlights with extra batteries easily accessible. Do not use candles due to fire risk. Keep the refrigerator closed to preserve food.
