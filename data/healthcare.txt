# Healthcare Guide for Seniors

## Medication Management
- **Adherence**: Take medications exactly as prescribed. Do not skip doses or double up.
- **Organization**: Use a pill organizer. Keep a written list of all current medications, dosages, and times.
- **Refills**: Request refills from your pharmacy at least 3-5 days before running out.

## Nutrition and Diet
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you don't feel thirsty. Aim for 8 glasses unless advised otherwise by a doctor.
- **Balanced Meals**: Focus on calcium, Vitamin D, and fiber. Eat plenty of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Reduce sodium intake.

## Physical Activity
- **Daily Movement**: Engage in at least 30 minutes of moderate activity daily, such as walking, swimming, or tai chi.
- **Fall Prevention**: Keep floors clear of clutter, use non-slip rugs, and install grab bars in the bathroom.

## When to See a Doctor
- If you experience a sudden, severe headache.
- If you feel persistent dizziness or loss of balance.
- If you notice sudden changes in vision or hearing.
- For routine checkups: Visit your primary care physician at least once a year.
