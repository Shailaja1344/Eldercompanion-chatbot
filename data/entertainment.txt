# Entertainment Guide for Seniors

## Television and Streaming
- **Smart TV Basics**: Use your remote to access apps like Netflix, YouTube, or PBS. Most smart TVs have a "Home" button that shows all apps.
- **Free Streaming**: YouTube, Pluto TV, and Tubi offer free movies and shows. PBS has excellent documentaries and nature programs.
- **Closed Captions**: If you have trouble hearing dialogue, turn on subtitles. Go to Settings > Accessibility > Subtitles on most TVs.

## Audiobooks and Podcasts
- **Libby App**: Connect your library card to the free Libby app to borrow audiobooks. No late fees, books return automatically.
- **Podcasts**: Try apps like Apple Podcasts or Spotify. Search for topics you enjoy: history, gardening, true crime, or news.
- **Audible**: Amazon's audiobook service offers a free trial. Listen while walking, gardening, or relaxing.

## Music
- **Spotify Free**: Listen to music for free with occasional ads. Search for your favorite artists or decades (e.g., "1960s Hits").
- **YouTube Music**: Search any song or artist on YouTube. Create playlists of your favorites.
- **Radio Apps**: iHeartRadio and TuneIn let you listen to radio stations from around the world for free.

## Brain Games and Puzzles
- **Crossword Puzzles**: Apps like "NYT Crossword" or printed puzzle books keep your mind sharp.
- **Sudoku**: Available as free apps or in newspapers. Great for logical thinking.
- **Lumosity**: A brain training app with memory, attention, and problem-solving games.
- **Jigsaw Puzzles**: Try the "Jigsaw Puzzle" app on your tablet for digital puzzles, or enjoy traditional ones.

## Hobbies and Activities
- **Gardening**: Even a small windowsill herb garden can be rewarding. Try basil, mint, or cherry tomatoes.
- **Arts and Crafts**: Painting, knitting, or scrapbooking. YouTube has free tutorials for beginners.
- **Reading Groups**: Many libraries host book clubs, both in-person and online via Zoom.
- **Bird Watching**: A simple bird feeder outside your window can provide hours of peaceful entertainment.

## Social Entertainment
- **Game Nights**: Card games, board games, or online games with family. Try "Words With Friends" to play with grandchildren.
- **Community Centers**: Many offer movie nights, dance classes, and social gatherings for seniors.
- **Virtual Tours**: Explore museums and landmarks from home. Google Arts & Culture offers free virtual museum tours.
