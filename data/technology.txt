# Technology Guide for Seniors

## Smartphone Basics
- **Making Calls**: Tap the green phone icon. Tap "Keypad" to dial a number, or "Contacts" to select someone you know.
- **Sending Messages**: Tap the green speech bubble icon. Enter the contact's name, type your message in the text box, and press the arrow to send.
- **Adjusting Volume**: Use the physical buttons on the side of the phone. You can also go to Settings > Sounds to adjust ringtone volume.

## Video Calling Family
- **WhatsApp**: Open WhatsApp, tap "Chats", select a contact, and tap the video camera icon at the top right.
- **FaceTime (Apple)**: Open the FaceTime app, tap "New FaceTime", enter a contact name, and tap the video button.
- **Zoom**: Download Zoom, click "Join a Meeting", and enter the meeting ID provided by your family.

## Internet Safety and Scams
- **Passwords**: Never share your passwords. Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
- **Phishing Emails**: Do not click on links in emails from unknown senders. If an email claims to be from your bank asking for personal info, call the bank directly instead.
- **Tech Support Scams**: Real companies will not call you out of the blue to fix your computer. Hang up if someone calls claiming your computer has a virus.
