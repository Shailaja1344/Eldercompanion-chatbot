# Banking and Finance Guide for Seniors

## Safe Banking Practices
- **Checking Accounts**: Review your bank statements every month for unauthorized charges. You can do this by mail or online.
- **Online Banking**: Only log in to your bank's official website. Look for the padlock icon in the address bar. Do not log in using public Wi-Fi.
- **ATMs**: Shield the keypad when entering your PIN. Do not accept help from strangers at an ATM.

## Understanding Pensions and Social Security
- **Social Security**: Benefits are usually deposited directly into your bank account on a specific date each month. Call the SSA at 1-800-772-1213 for inquiries.
- **Pensions**: Keep records of your pension statements. If you have questions about your payout, contact your former employer's HR department.

## Fraud Prevention
- **Grandparent Scam**: If someone calls claiming to be your grandchild in trouble and asks for money, hang up and call the grandchild or their parents directly.
- **Lottery Scams**: If you are told you won a lottery but have to pay a fee to get the prize, it is a scam.
- **Bank Impersonators**: Your bank will never text or call you to ask for your PIN, password, or full social security number.
